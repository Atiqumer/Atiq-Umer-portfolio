## Site architecture
- The homepage is one pinned scroll stage (`src/components/site/PhoneExperience.tsx`): a foldable device whose inner screen renders `ScreenContent` once per half, moved by a shared scroll-driven `y`. Why: the reference site is a scroll-to-unfold phone, and two synced copies let the hinge half rotate in 3D.
- In-screen reveal animations use `useScreenReveal` (scroll offset), not IntersectionObserver/whileInView. Why: IO misfires inside the 3D-rotated half and the two copies would fall out of sync.
- Layout inside the screen sizes with container units (`cqw`, `cqmin`) so the same sections work in the wide unfolded and narrow phone screens.
- Portfolio content lives in `src/data/` (profile, experience, skills, projects); sections read from there. `PORTRAIT_URL` in `src/data/services.ts` switches the hero/call avatar from the typographic fallback to the real photo. Why: one place to update content.
- Project list lives in `src/data/projects.ts` (`allProjects`); the homepage shows `featuredProjects` (first four) and `/projects` is a plain non-phone page listing all. Why: keep the phone experience short while every project stays reachable.
- Text sizes use the `.t-hero/.t-h2/.t-h3/.t-lead/.t-body/.t-meta` classes in `src/styles.css` (clamped container units). Why: one consistent, readable scale across the wide and narrow phone screens.
- The final contact cover owns its own enquiry steps and form state independently of the phone timeline; height-container styles fit the half-height mobile cover, and visibility/reduced-motion gates pause decorative activity. Why: call interactions must not alter folding or portfolio scrolling.
- Phone open/closed state drives `inert`: the cover is inert while open, the inner screens and bottom nav while closed; focus caught in a side that becomes inert moves to the active side. Why: hidden controls must never be keyboard- or screen-reader-reachable.
- Keyboard focus inside the open phone drives the scroll timeline (PhoneExperience focusin) and force-reveals entrance animations (useScreenReveal listens for focusin and a mirrored "force-reveal" event). Why: the phone screen is clipped, so native focus scrolling cannot reveal controls.
